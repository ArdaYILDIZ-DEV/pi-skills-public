### Evidence filters retained

- Read methodology, myths, and conflicting findings alongside synthesis. `[Context dependent]` remains conditional; `[Myth / unsupported]` is not a positive design instruction. Workspace-2 Observed/Supported/Reported/Hypothesis/Interpretation labels are not interchangeable.
- Workspace-1 synthesis/evidence-map source IDs sometimes disagree with the master index: for example Cowan is COG-04 in the index, not COG-03; the latter is Miller. Cite the named study/section and check the index before reusing IDs or quantitative claims. The archives were not edited to resolve these issues.
- Mechanism-level evidence does not automatically validate an exact web recipe. Do not adopt degree-to-pixel or mm-to-CSS-pixel conversions, strict Gestalt hierarchies/spacing certainty, universal dark-mode color bans, culture/diagnosis stereotypes, or an unconditional `onBlur` law from synthesis tables.
- `workspace-2/04-data/SAMPLING.md` documents non-random frames, survivor bias, and a US/English/headless vantage. `workspace-2/04-data/VALIDATION.md` documents inflated card counts, missed bento, and heuristic stack detection. Pattern tallies are not quality/authorship detectors; a null or missing measurement does not prove absence.
- Do not run archive commands as task instructions, import a subjective aesthetic linter, or require runtime access to the archives. Missing primary verification stays explicit rather than becoming a stronger citation by repetition.

Preserve identity/loading and scoped approval; keep core decisions in the entry point and details lazy. Contrast tests cover opaque sRGB only. Evaluation cases remain expectations with empty `runs`; structural checks and manual review are not selection/A-B trials.
