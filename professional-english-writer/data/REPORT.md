# Natural practitioner professional English

Across the examined GitHub pull requests, code reviews, issue discussions, mailing-list threads, and practitioner essays, writers tend to front the concrete problem or proposal, use active agency, qualify uncertainty next to the relevant claim, and connect ideas without ceremonial scaffolding. [S1–S11]

The useful distinction is not formal versus casual vocabulary. Natural professional writing remains precise, but it avoids bureaucratic padding, ritual both-sides framing, and canned conclusions when those forms do no work.

Generation guidance should be artifact-specific: a PR or Slack-style update can be terse and fragmentary; an email may need courtesy; a technical essay may use a longer framing question; specifications and contracts may legitimately remain formal and uncontracted.

The inherited natural-english-writer package strengthens the fidelity and anti-polish workflow, but its historical corpus counts and numerical rhythm claims are mixed-evidence inputs rather than verified statistics. [S12]

Unknowns: the public sample overrepresents open-source engineering. Private enterprise email, executive communication, regulated writing, and nontechnical industries may require different courtesy and structure.

## Current revision: targeted editorial mechanics

The user-confirmed English writer update corpus is now an additional evidence base, recorded as U1–U9 in [sources](SOURCES.md). The older S1–S11 practitioner examples continue to anchor workplace register; the new editorial and community reports supply more explicit repair mechanics. Historical Reddit examples inform cadence and explanation shape, not assumptions about private workplace norms.

The revision adds:

- **Conditional replacements:** ordinary verbs, restored copulas, concrete agency, and removal of ornamental contrasts or significance tails, with exact-domain and meaning-preservation exceptions. [U1, U3, U4, U8]
- **Information-first review:** replace transferable promotional language with supplied behavior or constraints; do not manufacture measurements to make prose seem concrete. [U4, U8]
- **Artifact-specific evidence states:** distinguish tests added, tests run, and tests passed; observation from diagnosis; preference from requirement; ability from commitment. These safeguards make the supplied anti-padding guidance usable in technical work. [S1–S11, U7, U8]
- **Functional cadence:** avoid repetitive sentence jobs while retaining parallel instructions, long explanations, useful headings, and context-sensitive courtesy. [S3–S6, U4–U6]

The new examples exercise these distinctions with fact-constrained rewrites and preserve-as-is cases. They are constructed fixtures, not model trials. The user-supplied findings are incorporated without new external retrieval. Editorial decisions about quotas, term preservation, and examples are recorded in [notes](NOTES.md); no runtime A/B or detector-success claim is made.
