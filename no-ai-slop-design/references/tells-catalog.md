# Contextual tells

For a full generic-pattern audit inspect every category; otherwise stay with affected roles. This is an investigation catalog, not a defect checklist. Require a local consequence supported by the task/content/brief before flagging a pattern; preserve a legitimate use or make a clean assessment. Classify findings as functional harm, content/domain mismatch, or optional taste. Mark predicted consequences as inferred, not observed task failures.

Pattern counts and effect combinations prove neither quality nor AI authorship. The research crawl used non-random frames and heuristic detectors: off-screen/repeated containers inflated card counts, bento recall was poor, and stack signatures were uncertain. Do not import its tally as a detector, a quality score, or a repair target. Plain, familiar, distinctive, accessible, and fast are independent properties. Explicit examples/fixtures are not defects in the real interface.

## Typography

| Inspect | Flag when | Smallest useful repair / valid use |
|---|---|---|
| Starter family/sizes or fashionable mixed-face accent | Text roles are flat or the accent substitutes for hierarchy | Tune size, weight, measure, and leading first. A single Inter/Geist/system family can work. |
| Blanket tight tracking, forced breaks, or balance rules | Text cramps or wraps poorly across widths/locales | Test real text/fonts; adjust role-specific measure/size. Verified deliberate breaks can stay. |
| Repeated eyebrows/pills | Redundant labels compete with the title or obscure meaningful status/category distinctions | Remove unnecessary competition; keep useful category/date/issue/filter metadata and fitting brand treatment. A decorative badge alone is not proof of harm. |
| Misaligned numbers or mixed fallback glyphs | Numeric scanning or locale reading breaks | Use tabular figures; verify actual glyph coverage, language, and casing. `unicode-range` cannot supply glyphs. |

## Color and surfaces

| Inspect | Flag when | Smallest useful repair / valid use |
|---|---|---|
| Purple gradients, cream/sage, cyan/dark, or glows | Their prominence conflicts with task hierarchy or an explicit brand/brief requirement | Preserve fitting brand seeds; repair role mapping, not hue by reflex. A familiar palette alone is not a defect; distinctiveness suggestions remain taste unless a brief mismatch is demonstrated. |
| Equal saturation/emphasis everywhere | Actions, statuses, and decoration compete | Distinguish roles/prominence. Multiple colors are valid in expressive systems. |
| Gradient/image/translucent text background | Actual contrast fails or varies unpredictably | Provide stable text surfaces/scrims or a verified treatment; inspect composited states. |
| Glass, grids, blobs, spotlights | They obscure content, suggest misleading relationships, or demonstrably impair rendering/interaction | Repair the affected layer; retain fitting motifs with readable fallbacks. Decorative purpose can be legitimate; source presence does not prove runtime cost. |
| Identical shadows or flat dark surfaces | Grouping/elevation is unclear | Tune surface/border/elevation roles. Black/white endpoints and stock focus colors can be deliberate. |

Check actual pairs, not classes or lightness differences. Muted metadata still needs applicable text contrast. Missing state shades matter; an unused 50–900 ramp does not.

## Composition

| Inspect | Flag when | Smallest useful repair / valid use |
|---|---|---|
| Badge + centered hero + equal CTA pair | Content obscures the offer or equally emphasizes actions with different task importance | Rank real actions and show supplied evidence. Centering can focus a singular offer; two equally important destinations can justify equal CTAs. Co-occurrence is not authorship evidence. |
| Stock section sequence, large footer, repeated CTA | Content does not support those sections/destinations | Reorder around understanding → evidence → decision; remove filler only. |
| Identical icon-card grids or nested panels | Equal weight contradicts task importance, or repeated enclosure obstructs comparison/uses work space without clarifying membership | Feature important proof; flatten only redundant panels. Comparable offers and heterogeneous units can justify cards; retain helpful common-region grouping. |
| Forced asymmetry/bento | Visual priority contradicts informational priority | Restore equality or choose spans from real importance, not novelty. |
| One width/spacing everywhere | Prose stretches, tables crush, groups lose distinction | Separate reading/work/media roles and group spacing. One container can suit a small page. |
| Dense tables or sparse summaries | Clutter obstructs the task, or disclosure hides values/state needed together | Remove redundant chrome first; preserve readable labels, units, state, and comparisons. Both structured density and sparse linear flows can fit; no fixed row/attribute quota. |

## Components, content, and motion

- **Primitive/default chrome:** flag demonstrated mismatch, not library use. Next.js, Tailwind, shadcn/ui, Inter, Geist, and Lucide do not determine visual quality. Theme only affected shared roles; retain working semantics, focus management, familiar placement, and consistent behavior. Do not make every instance unique or swap libraries as a taste repair.
- **Icons, stripes, badges:** remove redundant encoding; preserve useful recognition/status. Existing icons and account initials are legitimate; invented endorsements are not.
- **Happy-path-only UI:** inspect focus, selected, disabled, loading, empty, error, and validation states. Keep loading labels stable and entered values recoverable.
- **Mobile omissions:** exercise navigation and critical actions; check narrow slots as well as viewport width. Mobile text-entry generally needs ≥16px to avoid iOS auto-zoom. Never disable zoom, hide overflow, or delete features to disguise breakage.
- **Generic/fake media:** prefer relevant authorized output or diagrams. Photos are optional. Check crops, readable image text, alt text, and chart scales. Invented customer proof, misleading chart values, or false urgency are truth defects.
- **Generic copy:** explain what happens and for whom. Keep meaning and approved facts. Action labels must make destinations clear; punctuation/casing are not detector rules.
- **Blanket reveals/lifts/loops:** flag delayed reading, false affordances, distraction, or broken reduced-motion behavior. Functional transitions and fitting playful springs can stay; no motion is valid.
