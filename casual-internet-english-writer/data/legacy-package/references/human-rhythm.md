# Human-Rhythm

Read for drafts, revisions, and audits. This is an editorial procedure, not a measured speech model or authorship detector.

## Pass: meaning into readable beats

1. Identify what each sentence does: answer, reason, condition, detail, turn, or ending. A beat is a meaningful unit, not a fixed number of words.
2. Inspect consecutive sentences for repetitive shape or competing ideas. Length alone is not a fault. Split overload at a genuine boundary; retain the condition that limits each claim.
3. Rejoin fragments when they impede comprehension or perform emotion the user did not express. A short sentence can carry emphasis without one-word staccato. For a semicolon joining clauses, each side must stand as a complete sentence; restore a missing subject/verb or change the join.
4. Use a shared subject or a real logical link between beats. Do not sprinkle `And`, `But`, and `So` to meet a quota. In particular, do not imply cause from mere sequence.
5. Vary pace where the material earns it: a plain answer, a fuller explanation, then a useful detail. The same pattern need not recur in every paragraph.
6. Read the result for the intended recipient. Preserve natural looseness from user samples, not random grammatical damage. Finish when the communicative job is done.

## Constructed rhythm transformations

These examples preserve their own supplied facts. They are not Reddit quotes.

| Before | After | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| "I tried restarting the app, and it opened, and then it crashed again when I opened the same file, so I still can't edit it." | "I restarted the app. It opened, but it crashed again when I opened the same file. I still can't edit it." | Separate recovery, recurrence, and current state; keep the trigger |
| "The export failed. Again. Same file. Same error." | "The export failed again with the same file and error." | Remove unsupported theatrical pacing |
| "I can come on Friday if the train is running." | Unchanged. | Condition already belongs to the promise |
| "This approach is slower. It preserves the original files." | "This approach is slower, but it preserves the original files." | Contrast made explicit, not causal |
| "I do not want another replacement." | Unchanged if the speaker wants emphasis. | Contractions are not compulsory |
| "Not because I forgot; I was ill." | "It wasn't because I forgot. I was ill." | Restore the complete denied-cause clause; retain the actual reason |

## What not to operationalize

Do not compute BPR, STALL, RELEASE, case multipliers, or serial-decay coefficients from the historical notes. They are hand-authored heuristics without a validated measurement procedure or fit evidence. Do not enforce their thresholds, the notes' maximum sentence lengths, a mandatory one-third connector rate, or an absence-of-word rule.

Do not use the number of ellipsis dots to infer a person's emotional state. Do not add a joke, analogy, brand, number, quotation, or fragment when the brief supplies no reason for one. Better rhythm is a clearer rendering of the same message, not extra content.

## Final rhythm check

Check first-read clarity, conditions, useful longer sentences, and a brief-appropriate ending. Different cadence never licenses different facts.
