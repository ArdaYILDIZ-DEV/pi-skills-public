# Turkish text into natural English

Read for Turkish-to-English translation or composition, not literal substitution, summarization, or persona changes. Examples are constructed contrasts, not corpus quotations or fixed answers.

## 1. Separate message from instructions

Identify the user-authored text, notes about what to say, quoted recipient/thread context, and drafting instructions. Only intended message content goes into the finished English.

- “İngilizceye çevir: Neden tekrar ödeme yapmam gerekiyor?” becomes “Why do I have to pay again?” Do not explain billing to the user.
- “Ona cevap yaz: bugün olmaz, yarın olabilir. Kibar olsun.” becomes a polite reply about availability. Do not include “Make it polite,” and do not repeat the other person's message as the user's own.
- “Başlık istemiyorum,” “özetleme,” and “açıklama ekleme” govern the artifact. A genuinely intended public boundary such as “Tavsiye aramıyorum” can belong in the message; decide from the brief rather than deleting or publishing every such phrase mechanically.

Sufficient text needs no platform question. Notes permit composition, not new events, reasons, or opinions. Necessary questions follow the instruction language, not the translation language; no bilingual duplicates.

## 2. Preserve meaning before surface form

Track actor, action, time, negation, conditions, quantities, attribution, and the degree of commitment. Keep emotionally important distinctions even when close English words seem interchangeable.

| Turkish distinction | Preserve in English | Do not silently substitute |
|---|---|---|
| `yapabilirim` / `yapacağım` | Possibility or ability / commitment, as context determines | An unconditional promise for a tentative offer |
| `yapabilirdim` | Past or counterfactual possibility | A current promise |
| `sanırım`, `galiba`, `olabilir` | Relevant uncertainty near its claim | A settled diagnosis or fact |
| `istemiyorum` / `sevmiyorum` / `nefret ediyorum` | Not wanting / not liking / hating | The same negative sentiment for all three |
| `uğraşmak istemiyorum` / `umurumda değil` | Unwillingness to spend effort / lack of concern | Indifference when the user is merely tired of trying |
| `belli değil` / `henüz kesin değil` | Unknown or unclear / not certain yet, as context determines | A claim that no schedule or decision exists merely because the speaker is unsure |
| `bende oluyor` | Personal observation | A universal product defect |
| `şimdilik`, `henüz`, `artık`, `yine`, `de` | Relevant temporary scope, timing, recurrence, or inclusion | Dropping them simply to shorten the text |

Do not automatically map every `-miş` to “apparently.” It can mark reported information, inference, or realization; preserve the function supported by context. If the speaker only heard a claim, do not present it as firsthand knowledge. Turkish can omit gender: use singular “they,” a role, or natural restructuring rather than inventing “he,” “she,” a partner, or a spouse. Preserve ambiguity that English can safely preserve; ask only when the recipient needs a distinction that cannot be recovered.

Keep negation scope: “Bunun işe yaramadığını söylemiyorum” is not “This doesn't work.” Keep willingness distinct from ability, and a request distinct from a demand. A caveat can move next to its claim but cannot disappear.

## 3. Rebuild the English

Rebuild English sentence order and clauses; preserve paragraph development unless restructuring is requested. Natural does not mean short.

- Render idioms by function. `İçime sinmedi` may be “I'm not convinced,” “I'm not happy with it,” or “It doesn't feel right,” depending on what the speaker doubts. It is not one fixed English phrase.
- `Ya`, `işte`, `hani`, `yani`, and `açıkçası` can mark emphasis, hesitation, explanation, or contrast. Keep the effect when it matters; do not mechanically add “well,” “you know,” “I mean,” “honestly,” or `tbh` for every particle.
- Favor ordinary combinations such as “raise an issue,” “get a refund,” or “make a decision” where they fit. Do not preserve a Turkish verb-noun combination that sounds unnatural in English. Check repeated event nouns too: “the launch is launching” is not natural phrasing. Distinguish an update about an event from the event itself.
- Rebuild tangled negative contrasts without changing which explanation is rejected. A noun phrase can avoid “wasn't not being...” when it preserves the same contrast. For example, constructed `Sorun hatırlamamam değildi, vakit bulamamamdı` can become `Remembering wasn't the problem. Finding time was.` Do not turn a denied cause into an affirmed one.
- Keep technical terms, names, versions, UI labels, prices, units, and exact quotations as required. Do not convert currency, calculate a return deadline, or invent a year during a writing task.
- Maintain intensity and register. Do not add apologies to firm refusals, therapeutic reassurance to complaints, or slang to otherwise plain prose. English samples calibrate English-specific choices; Turkish source tone still counts when samples are absent.

## 4. Two short reviews, not two displayed drafts

**Read only the English:** fix awkward collocations, accidental repetition, comma splices, excessive framing, and forced fragments. Use ordinary sentence joins and contractions. Do not turn “taking a short break for a little while” into a rhythm pattern to imitate.

**Compare with Turkish:** preserve every material fact, condition, stance, uncertainty, and attribution; check additions as well as omissions. Rephrase rather than sacrifice meaning. Return only finished text unless analysis is requested.

## Constructed input/output contrasts

| Input | One faithful rendering | Why the tempting alternative fails |
|---|---|---|
| “Haklı olduğunu söylemiyorum, sadece neden böyle düşündüğünü anlıyorum.” | “I'm not saying you're right. I just understand why you see it that way.” | “You're right, I get it” invents agreement. |
| “Salı uğrayabilirim, toplantı erken biterse.” | “I might stop by Tuesday if the meeting ends early.” | “I'll see you Tuesday” deletes the condition and strengthens commitment. |
| “Bu kadar beklemeye değdi mi, hâlâ emin değilim.” | “I'm still not sure it was worth the wait.” | “It wasn't worth the wait” removes uncertainty. |
| “Çalışmıyor demiyorum. Her açışımda yeniden giriş istemesi can sıkıcı.” | “I'm not saying it doesn't work. It's annoying that it asks me to log in every time I open it.” | “It's broken and unusable” replaces a specific annoyance with a verdict. |
| “Onun da haberi yokmuş.” | “It turns out they didn't know either.” | “He lied about it” invents gender, knowledge, and motive. |
| “Bunu İngilizce yaz: bugün gelemem. Özür ekleme.” | “I can't come today.” | “Sorry, I can't make it” violates the requested stance. |

These are examples of decisions, not phrases to reuse everywhere. Use the [voice guide](voice-and-register.md) for register and the [Reddit guide](reddit-writing.md) when the artifact is a post or comment.
