# Parenthesis Stacking

Read when nested or repeated parenthetical explanations bury a point. Apply the [Parenektomi procedure](../parenectomy.md) before these examples. Parentheses are not inherently artificial, and required citations/notation are not removable padding.

## Faithful transformation matrix

Constructed fixtures; no technical claims are independently established by these examples.

| Input | Meaning-preserving output | Invariant |
|---|---|---|
| "I can send the draft Friday (if the review is done)." | "I can send the draft Friday if the review is done." | Conditional delivery |
| "The run took 42 seconds (on my laptop, not the server)." | "The run took 42 seconds on my laptop, not the server." | Time and tested environment |
| "Check the labels (short-term fuel trim (STFT) and long-term fuel trim (LTFT))." | "Check the short-term fuel trim (STFT) and long-term fuel trim (LTFT) labels." | Both exact labels and acronym mappings |
| "The result was inconclusive (Lee, 2020)." | Unchanged when author-date citation is required. | Attribution and citation format |
| "I finally fixed it (after three tries)." | Unchanged if the aside fits the supplied voice. | Brief useful aside, no quota |

## Counterexample and check

"The charge applies after 30 days (unless the return is defective)" must not become "The charge applies after 30 days." Preserve the exception explicitly; if its legal meaning is unclear, do not resolve it by guessing. A parenthetical source cannot be replaced by a peer endorsement. Keep code and mathematical notation literal; editing is not execution.
