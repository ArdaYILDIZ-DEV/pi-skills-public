# Voice samples: illustrations, not user history

Read only when calibration needs an illustration; current user samples win. These five legacy examples have undocumented origins, not verified Reddit provenance or user biography.

Different registers are not ingredients to combine: borrow no facts, typos, or slang mix. Without matching samples, follow the [voice guide](../references/voice-and-register.md).

## Preserved legacy samples

### Keyboard review

> tbh i bought this keyboard for the hype and now im stuck with it. switches are fine but the software is actual garbage, had to use some random github tool just to remap a key. wouldnt buy again

Decision illustrated: a personal verdict followed by a concrete complaint. The keyboard purchase, software failure, and tool use cannot become the user's biography unless supplied.

### DM

> yo are you free this weekend? thinking of going to that new place, lmk if you're down. no stress if not

Decision illustrated: an invitation with a low-pressure ending. This register fits only an established relationship and supported voice.

### Social post

> people really out here paying 100$ for a shirt with a tiny logo and calling it fashion lol. meanwhile my 10$ plain one does the same job

Decision illustrated: a casual contrast. Prices and a personal purchase are not reusable facts; the sample does not prescribe unconventional currency placement.

### Gaming

> just spent 4 hours on this boss and died at 1% health twice. ngl im about to uninstall. game is good tho, i'll be back tomorrow

Decision illustrated: frustration followed by a genuine reversal. Do not insert a reversal into a serious complaint that has none.

### Work

> spent the whole day on a bug that was literally one missing semicolon. i hate it here. well not really, the job is fine lol

Decision illustrated: a comic walk-back. It is not a universal pattern for workplace messages and not evidence that the user encountered this bug.

## Constructed calibration counterexamples

- To an unfamiliar supplier: "Could you send the revised invoice by Friday?" Keep this polite request; the gaming sample does not justify `yo` or `lol`.
- A user's firm complaint: "I don't want another replacement. I want a refund." Do not add "well, not really" or apologize for the stance.
- No supplied biography: "Write a comment about choosing a nearby gym." Discuss convenience without claiming that the user switched gyms or doubled attendance.

The [faithful-rewrite examples](faithful-rewrites.md) demonstrate preserving these constraints. The exact original entry point, including older illustrative before/after pairs that add facts, is preserved as [historical text](../data/archive/original-skill.txt), not operational instruction.
