## 1. Aggregate frequency data (observed ranges, not population estimates)

All figures are in-sample tendencies from the coded pool. Denominators differ by question (openers coded per sentence-initial position; tails per finite clause; punctuation per body). Ranges reflect variation across the 20 threads, not measurement error around a single mean.

### 1.1 Sentence-length regimes by genre

| Genre | Threads (primary) | Typical sentence | Punch unit | Long tail | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wit (one-liners, puns) | S10, S5, S15 | 8–14 words, often single-sentence turns | 3–6 words | almost never >18 | "All resources are limited." (S5); "What's the largest mammal you think you could knock out with a single punch?" (S15) |
| Anecdote (stories, confessions) | S1, S8, S13, S17, S18 | punch 4–6 + unpack 10–16 | ≤6 | 20–24 only at climax | "I was a sheltered kid." + unpack (S1); "Yeah I cheated." + flat elaboration (S13) |
| Explainer (lay science, expert-lay) | S2, S7, S11 | 12–20 words | 5–8 (analogy hook) | 22–28 inside procedures | "Think of a garden hose…" (S2); "Antioxidants are like gas for your car." (S7) |
| Tip / instruction | S3, S11-short, S19 | 6–12 words, imperative-heavy | 2–5 ("Walk away.", "RUN AWAY.") | length carried by lists, not sentences | "In Chrome, press Ctrl+Shift+c…" (S3); "suck it up, buttercup." (S19) |
| Memoir / relationship / grief | S9, S12, S14, S20 | 10–18 words, paratactic (*and*-chains, commas) | 4–7 ("Life is good.", "Now I'm sad.") | 25+ only as *and*-chains, never nested subordination | "It's been beautiful, exhausting, exhilarating…" (S9); "My mum was 14 when she met my dad. He was 24." (S14, 11 words total) |
| Boundary / dating-receipt | S16 | 8–18 words + period-fragments | 1–3 ("Gotcha.", "Yep.") | detail stacks, then walkout line | "He. Ate. Everything. In. Front. Of. Him. Ok. Gotcha." (S16) |

Hard constraints observed (zero counterexamples in top comments): never three 20+ word sentences in a row; every long post (S9 ~700 words, S11 ~2,500, S18 ~900) contains multiple ≤6-word sentences; fragments are load-bearing ("count chocula" S6; "And walked out." S8).

### 1.2 Opener distribution (sentence-initial position)

- Bare S-V start ~35%: "I don't remember her exact phrasing…" (S1); "Yeah I cheated." (S13).
- And / But / So ~25%: "And you better save frequently because…" (S5); sentence-initial *And/But/So* is the default cohesion device.
- When / If / Because ~15%: "When Skyrim came out…" (S5); "If fluids are low…" (S11).
- Discourse markers (Well / Look / To be fair / Frankly / Not really) ~10%; vocatives (Man / Dude / OP / You guys) ~8%, concentrated at reply level; interjections (Oh / Wtf / Ooh / Damn / Fuck) ~5%.
- Imperative openers ~12% in tips/explainers, ~3% elsewhere.
- Academic openers (Moreover / Furthermore / Additionally / Firstly / It is important to note): **0 occurrences** in the coded pool.

### 1.3 Clause shapes and tails

- Main-clause shapes: S-V-O declarative (majority), imperative (tips), fragment (wit/reply), quoted speech (climax), list-item (procedures).
- Reason folded **inside** the sentence via *because/when/if*-tail (file 02 T2) rather than a separate "This is because…" sentence.
- Prepositional tails ground verbs in body/tool/place (*with your thumb, on the toilet, in my defense, at once*); abstract tails (*with regard to, in terms of, in the context of*): 0.
- Hedges placed **retroactively inside** the same sentence (*just / really / literally / frankly / pretty / kind of / to be fair / in my defense*), never as a separate disclaimer paragraph.
- Revision layer appended visibly (*Edit: / ETA: / Hold on / TL;DR*) instead of silent rewriting — present in S3, S4, S9, S11, S14, S18.

### 1.4 Punctuation and case deviations (descriptive baseline; formal models pending in F2-02)

Attested, with function (no invented frequencies):

- Fragments and period-fragments for emphasis and pacing: "And walked out." (S8); "He. Ate. Everything." (S16).
- Ellipsis as breath/hesitation, especially grief: "It's ok to ......... it's OK,..... I'll be fine" (S14).
- Dashes (`--` / em-dash) for mid-sentence pivot and overflow: paratactic chains in S9 ("beautiful, exhausting, exhilarating… — just an overall whirlwind"); interruption in dialogue-heavy S8/S12.
- Lowercase starts and unedited casing as register signal: "quit a job i absolutely hated." (S6); lowercase-*i* throughout S9; unapostrophized *dont / wasnt / ive* left standing.
- Caps as severity/urgency scale, not shouting at random: "RUN AWAY." vs "Walk away." (S11); "OH CRAP" (S4); "HE SHOWED!" (S18).
- Parentheses reserved for asides/mutters/punchlines (file 03 B2), never acronym-first definitions.
- No formulas are asserted here; sequence/rhythm modeling is reserved for the pending 02 expansion (T10–T15). Any formula encountered elsewhere without a stated scope and fit statistic should be treated as provisional.

## 3. General writing rules (normative; each cites its data)

**R1 — Open with punch, unpack with props.** First sentence ≤6 words where possible (T1); second sentence carries one number, place, brand, body part, or quoted fragment. Background-first openings ("During my formative years…") are rewritten or deleted. *Data: §1.1, §2-T1.*

**R2 — Keep the reason inside the sentence.** Stance/explanation = [conjunction + short claim + because/when/if-tail] in one sentence (T2). Splits into "…wall. This is because…" are rejoined; *However,* + passive is replaced by *But/So* + active. *Data: §1.3, §2-T2.*

**R3 — Scope first, point second (instructional contexts).** *In/When/If*-fronted scope, then the point (T3). *Data: §2-T3.*

**R4 — Instruct verb-first; let the tail carry rationale.** Bare imperative + object + *to/and/then*-tail (T4); politeness via *just*, never *please kindly / users are advised*. One concrete noun per instruction (tool, body part, household object). *Data: §1.1-tip row, §2-T4.*

**R5 — Explain by pointing, not by stacking.** The analogy *is* the definition (T8): hose, gas tank, eyeballs, milkshake, magnet, oven mitt. Taxonomy-first paragraphs are inverted to object-first. *Data: §2-T8; file 03 Family C.*

**R6 — Soften after the spine, inside the sentence.** Claim first, then ≤5-word hedge (T5) from the ladder *just → pretty → kind of → really/literally → frankly → to be fair → in my defense*. Sentence-initial throat-clearing ("I might be wrong but…") and standalone disclaimer sentences are deleted or folded in. *Data: §1.3, §2-T5.*

**R7 — Climax with quoted speech; compress with TL;DR.** Payoff lines 3–10 words as their own unit (T7); TL;DR keeps names, verdicts, feelings and drops dates/caveats/methods (S8, S12, S17). Summarizing dialogue instead of staging it is rewritten. *Data: §2-T6/T7.*

**R8 — Stop; append, don't summarize.** End on detail, joke, thanks, blessing, number, quote, or *Edit:/ETA:/Hold on* (T6); *in conclusion / ultimately / this underscores* closers are deleted, with one fact moved up if information would be lost. Live repair is appended visibly. *Data: §1.1-hard constraints, §2-T6.*

**R9 — Subjects are people; verbs are actable.** Prefer *I/you/we* over *it/one/users/individuals*; contractions near-obligatory in personal action (*don't, can't, I'm, it's*); phrasal verbs over Latinate (*figure out, walk away, hold on*); deliberate vagueness (*a ton of, kind of thing*) paired with precise experience-numbers (*5 weeks, 3rd graders, :55 secs, 10%*). *Data: §1.2–1.3; file 02 §4/§6.*

**R10 — Punctuation carries rhythm; use sparingly and functionally.** Fragments for punch; period-fragments only for staged emphasis (S16); ellipsis only for hesitation/breath (S14), not trailing thought; dashes for pivot/overflow (S9); caps only on the severity scale (S11); parentheses only for asides/mutters/punchlines, never definitions (file 03 B4). Uncontracted, fully-punctuated stiffness in personal narration is treated as a synthetic signal. *Data: §1.4; file 02 §6.*

## 4. Prohibited patterns (zero or near-zero in pool; rewrite on sight)

From files 02 §6 and 03 Families A–C (D–E pending; not asserted here):

1. Essay scaffolding: *moreover, furthermore, additionally, firstly/secondly, in conclusion, in summary, it is important/should be noted.*
2. Dummy-authority subjects + nominalizations + passive personal action (*users are advised; implementation/utilization/disbursement piles*); Latinate verbs with phrasal alternatives (*utilize, ascertain, facilitate, mitigate*); boosters (*crucial, vital, exceptional, sincerely*).
3. Definitional/hedge/citation parentheses: acronym-first expansions, *(depending on…)* hedge-piles, *(see…; cf.…)* piles. Parenectomy rule (file 03 B4): definition → new short sentence or deletion; keep parens only for asides/punchlines.
4. Noun stacks (≥3: *thermal differential dynamics*) and preposition chains (*in the context of / with regard to / in terms of*); double-hedged passives (*may be considered potentially indicative of*).
5. Summary closers and hedging closers (*I hope this comprehensive overview proves valuable*); silent-rewrite posture where an *Edit:/ETA:* appendage is due.
6. Triple-long stacking (3×20+ words with no fragment/joke/hedge/interjection); paragraphs of personal action with zero contractions.

## 6. Verification checklist (pre-publish; general, no schedule attached)

1. ≥1 sentence starts *And/But/So*; ≥1 sentence ≤6 words.
2. Longest sentence ≤22 words (procedure steps ≤18 each); no triple-long stacking.
3. Zero items from §4.1–4.2; subject is *I/you*, not *it/one/users*.
4. Every *because/when/if* reason sits inside its claim sentence (R2); no "This is because…" seconds.
5. Every instruction is verb-first with a concrete noun (R4); no *utilize/users are advised*.
6. Every abstraction has a household-object anchor or scene/number/quote (R5); parens hold no definitions (R10).
7. Hedges ≤5 words and inside the sentence (R6); no disclaimer paragraph.
8. Ending is joke/thanks/blessing/number/quote/Edit (R8); no summary paragraph.
9. Contractions present in personal action; caps/parens/ellipsis/dashes each functional or removed.
10. Quoted passages traceable to S-ids in file 01; uncertain or memory-based claims removed.
