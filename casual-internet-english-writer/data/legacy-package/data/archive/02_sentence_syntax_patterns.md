# 02 — Sentence & Syntax Patterns (Language Engine Edition, n≈520+)

**Scope:** All 20 threads in file 01 (S1–S20). Every example is verbatim (typos, caps, lowercase-*i* preserved) with source ID. Frequencies are **in-sample tendencies across ~520+ coded sentences**, ranked and ranged — never false-precise population percentages. Templates T1–T9 carried from Deep Edition; **T10–T15 new** (stream-of-consciousness punctuation: dash `--`, ellipsis `...`, lowercase starts, period-fragments). Rhythm formulas in §7 are **descriptive models with stated assumptions**, not population parameter estimates.

**One-sentence thesis:** Pre-2021 Reddit writes in **4–18 word bursts**, opens every third sentence with *And/But/So/When/If*, folds the reason into a *because/when/if*-tail **inside** the same sentence, gives advice verb-first, explains by pointing at household objects, stages payoffs as quoted speech — and under load (grief, shame, fury, love) **breaks punctuation first**: fragments multiply, periods invade mid-phrase, ellipses stall, dashes pivot, caps and lowercase trade off. Rhythm *is* the meaning.

## 1. Length regimes: seven genres, seven rhythms

| Genre | Threads | Typical sentence | Punch unit | Long tail | Verbatim proof |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wit (one-liners, puns, joke-questions) | S10, S5, S15 | 8–14 words, single-sentence turns | 3–6 words | almost never >18 | "All resources are limited." (S5); "What's the largest mammal you think you could knock out with a single punch?" (S15) |
| Anecdote / confession | S1, S8, S13, S17 | punch 4–6 + unpack 10–16 | ≤6 ("Yeah I cheated.") | 20–24 only at climax | "I was a sheltered kid." (S1); "Yeah I cheated. I didn't write any of my papers…" (S13) |
| Explainer (lay science, expert-lay) | S2, S7, S11 | 12–20 words | 5–8 (analogy hook) | 22–28 inside procedures | "Think of a garden hose…" (S2); "Antioxidants are like gas for your car." (S7) |
| Tip / instruction | S3, S11-short, S19 | 6–12 words, imperative | 2–5 ("Walk away.") | lists, not sentences | "In Chrome, press Ctrl+Shift+c…" (S3); "suck it up, buttercup." (S19) |
| Memoir / grief / tenderness | S9, S12, S14, S20 | 10–18 words, paratactic | 4–7 ("Now I'm sad.") | 25+ only as *and*-chains | "My mum was 14 when she met my dad. He was 24." (S14, 11 words total); "all I wanted was for someone to watch the movie with me." (S20) |
| Boundary / dating-receipt | S16 | 8–18 words + period-fragments | 1–3 ("Gotcha.") | receipt stacks, then walkout | "He. Ate. Everything. In. Front. Of. Him. Ok. Gotcha." (S16) |
| Serial / heroic-fail epic | S18 (+S9/S11 EDIT-chains) | 8–16 words, present-tense beats | interjection ("HE SHOWED!") | 900-word total carried by EDIT appendages, never one long sentence | "My brain blue-screened while I tread water." (S18) |

**Hard rules (zero counterexamples in top comments):** never three 20+ word sentences in a row; every long post holds multiple ≤6-word sentences; fragments load-bearing ("count chocula" S6; "And walked out." S8; "TLDR: Read the whole thing").

## 2. Openers: the 8-item inventory (+ case signal)

Bare S-V ~35% ("Yeah I cheated." S13); And/But/So ~25%; When/If/Because ~15%; discourse markers ~10%; vocatives ~8% (reply-level); interjections ~5%; imperatives ~12% tips / ~3% elsewhere; academic openers **0**. **New:** lowercase starts (*quit a job i absolutely hated.* S6; lowercase-*i* S9) function as intimacy/casualty markers, concentrated in confession and memoir; caps starts (*OH CRAP, HE SHOWED, RUN AWAY*) mark urgency/severity, never random shouting.

## 3. Templates T1–T9 (carried; witnesses extended to S13–S20)

Notation `[X]` = slot; each attested in ≥3 threads.

### T1 — [Punch ≤6 words.] + [Unpack 10–16 words with concrete prop]
"I was a sheltered kid." + unpack (S1); "All resources are limited." (S5); "Yeah I cheated." + "I didn't write any of my papers…" (S13).

### T2 — [Giriş Bağlacı: But / So / And] + [Kısa Ana Cümle ≤12 words] + [Niteleyici Yan Cümle: because / when / if …]
"And you better save frequently **because** …unplug it from the wall." (S5); "…**but** I somehow completely misunderstood **when** my mom told me about condoms." (S1).

### T3 — [Fronted Scope: When / If / In … ,] + [Main Clause]
"**When** you do a 'hoo'…" (S2); "**If** fluids are low…" (S11); "**In** Chrome, press…" (S3).

### T4 — [Bare Imperative] + [Object] + [Purpose tail: to … / and you will … / then …]
"Put your finger right in front your mouth **when** doing a 'hoo' **and** you will sense…" (S2); "Tell us what drugs and alcohol you're on. We aren't gonna tell the cops." (S7).

### T5 — [Plain Claim] + [Retroactive hedge INSIDE the same sentence]
"…**but frankly** I haven't had a ton of success…" (S3); "It's **pretty** common on newer cars…" (S11); "I probed a little more…" (S12).

### T6 — [Point ≤18 words. STOP.] + [Afterthought: Edit: / ETA: / Hold on / Thanks / Joke]
"Don't forget to wash your hands you animals. **Edit: thank you kind benefactors!**" (S2); "**OH CRAP**… hold on." (S4); "ETA: Thank you all so much…" (S14).

### T7 — [Buildup in past tense] + [Quoted-speech climax as own unit]
"'**I'm the guy who bought you dinner.**'" (S8); "'**Back of the queue.**'" (S8); "I stood up and said, '**I don't live here anymore… I'm going home.**' **And walked out.**" (S8).

### T8 — [Hard thing] + [Analogy bridge: Think of / It's like / Same goes for / Imagine …]
"Think of a garden hose…" (S2); "Antioxidants are like gas for your car." (S7).

### T9 — [Numbered/bulleted procedure] + [Walk-away closer]
"Walk away." ×11 / "RUN AWAY." (S11); "So how does it work? …* Income: You use this to…" (S4); "1) … 2) … 3) …" (S12).

## 4. Templates T10–T15 (new: social-load syntax + punctuation-first rhythm)

Each attested in ≥3 threads (witnesses listed); punctuation behavior is part of the template, modeled formally in §7.

### T10 — [Receipt stack 2–4 short sentences] + [Walkout line]
> "He proceeded to text on his phone the entire time at dinner, ignored me and took pics of his food… He ignored the waiter, plus the bill… I just pretended I was on my phone and left him there, and walked out the door." (S16)
> "'So you're a teacher right?' 'Yep' …… 'Well that's cool what grade?' '2nd' …… 'uh huh' ……check please!" (S16)
- *Function:* evidence before verdict; walkout is ≤8 words. Siblings: S8 ("And walked out."), S11 ("move on to the next car.").
- *Punctuation:* receipt sentences plain periods; walkout often fragment or with *…* stall before it.

### T11 — [Trigger] + [Intrusive image, present tense] + [Self-indictment]
> "…the image of her getting dicked down… would pop in my head killing my mood… I think back to the moment where I wrote my own death sentence by asking questions I didn't want to know the answer to." (S17)
> "I told her through tears that it was OK to die. I could not get the words out completely…" (S14, grief variant)
- *Function:* rumination loop; present-tense image interrupts past-tense narration. *like*-hedges ("Like there's no way I can measure up.") mark the loop entry.

### T12 — [Flat confession, no hedge] + [Peer salt/fatalism reply]
> "Yeah I cheated. I didn't write any of my papers, I didn't do any of the work myself… I feel a little bad but not really.." (S13)
> → "Keep that saltiness close to your heart because being in the workforce is more of the same." (S13 reply)
- *Function:* guilt without scaffolding; replies answer with fatalism, not absolution. Double-period (`..`) as shrug-punctuation.

### T13 — [Ellipsis-breath: clause trails `...`/`.........` → restart → lowercase continuation]
> "I said 'it's ok. I will be OK. It's ok to ......... it's OK,..... I'll be fine, it's OK to.....' I couldn't even say it." (S14)
> "I got a bit choked up because I really am. But I told her 'only sometimes'." (S20, mild variant: short sentence as held breath)
- *Function:* unsayable content marked orthographically; the restart (*it's OK,..... I'll be fine*) is quieter (often lowercase-adjacent) than the onset. Dots count emotion: 5+ dots = abandonment; 3 dots = pause-and-continue.

### T14 — [Period-fragment staccato] + [Dash pivot `--`/em-dash to verdict]
> "He. Ate. Everything. In. Front. Of. Him. Ok. Gotcha." (S16)
> "It's been beautiful, exhausting, exhilarating, very mentally and emotionally taxing — just an overall whirlwind…" (S9)
> "My brain blue-screened while I tread water. The lake felt infinitely deep." (S18, fragment-adjacent beats)
- *Function:* periods slow time to one word per beat (contempt, disbelief); dash then releases the held pressure into summary/verdict. Reverse (dash-first) marks interruption rather than release.

### T15 — [Post] + [Serial EDIT/ETA appendages as real-time diary]
> "EDIT: It's Lake Jocassee… EDIT2: Okay, at work now… EDIT5: HE SHOWED!… typing this from the bathroom… EDIT6: …He's getting the rest of my attention." (S18, 7 EDITs)
> "EDIT: the response… has been absolutely INCREDIBLE… i'm sitting in the office alone…" (S9); "ETA: Thank you all so much… ETA: I'm doing better…" (S14)
- *Function:* revision as serialization; each EDIT is a new timestamped beat with its own opener/punch. AI rewrites silently; humans append visibly — the most AI-distinctive textual feature in the corpus.

## 5. Conjunctions, prepositions, tails (carried + social-load notes)

Inventory ≈8 (*and, but, so, because, when, if, then, also*); polysyndeton/asyndeton mark overflow (S9). Tails ground in body/tool/place; abstract tails zero. Vague-quantity (*a ton of, kind of thing*) pairs with experience-numbers (*5 weeks, 3rd graders, 95th percentile, $100 vs $30*). Phrasal verbs dominate (*walk away, hold on, show up, blow up*). Deictics point at the thread (*this, that, right now, hold on*). **Addenda:** dating threads add money/phone deictics (*the bill, on his phone, split our bill*); grief threads add breath deictics (*through tears, at her side, with me at her side*).

## 6. Reply-level syntax (carried)

Shorter + vocative; agreement + one-fact increment; joke escalation by shortening. Social-load replies add comfort-minimalism ("Now I'm sad." S20; "Being a kid can be so lonely sometimes!" S20) and fatalism ("more of the same." S13) — validation without advice-stacking.

## 7. Rhythm formulas: how `--`, `...`, lowercase shape pace (descriptive models)

**Status:** heuristic beat models fitted by hand to cited passages. Assumptions stated; no population inference. Symbols: unit *i* = orthographic sentence/fragment; *lᵢ* = word count; terminal pause weight *pᵢ*: plain period 1.0; period-fragment 1.4; comma-splice boundary 0.4; dash pivot 0.6 (release) or 1.2 (interruption, context-decided); ellipsis ≤3 dots 1.5, >3 dots 2.2 + restart discount 0.7 on next unit's effective length; lowercase-continued unit ×0.8 length (de-emphasis); caps unit ×1.3 impact (not length).

- **F1 Burst–pause ratio:** `BPR = Σlᵢ / Σpᵢ`. Low BPR (≲6) = staccato (contempt/shock); mid (6–12) = conversational; high (>12) = overflow/memoir.
  - Worked: "He. Ate. Everything. In. Front. Of. Him. Ok. Gotcha." → l=[1,1,1,1,1,1,1,1,1], p≈1.4×9=12.6 → BPR≈0.7 (extreme staccato; contempt enacted, S16).
  - Worked: "My mum was 14 when she met my dad. He was 24." → l=[9,2] (approx by whitespace), p=[1.0,1.0] → BPR≈5.5 (flat compression; grief without ornament, S14).
- **F2 Stall–restart:** `STALL = (dots/3) × 1.5 − 0.7·restart_quiet`, where restart_quiet=1 if next unit shorter/quieter. "It's ok to ......... it's OK,..... I'll be fine" → dots≈9 → (3.0)×1.5=4.5 −0.7 → 3.8 pause-beats: abandonment, then quieter restart (S14).
- **F3 Dash release:** `RELEASE = l_before × 0.6 → l_after` reads as exhale when dash follows a list ("beautiful, exhausting, exhilarating… — just an overall whirlwind", S9: long l_before, short verdict after). Interruption variant (`p=1.2`) when dash cuts a clause mid-stream (dialogue overlaps, S8/S12).
- **F4 Case envelope:** lowercase continuation multiplies perceived volume ×0.8 ("quit a job i absolutely hated." S6; S9 *i*); caps multiplies ×1.3 on ≤4-word units ("HE SHOWED!", "RUN AWAY.") and reads as alarm only when rare — more than one caps unit per ~50 words collapses the effect (never observed in top comments).
- **F5 Serial-decay (EDIT chains):** successive EDIT beats shorten: `l_EDIT(n+1) ≈ 0.8·l_EDIT(n)` with rising interjection density (S18 EDIT1→EDIT5: exposition → "HE SHOWED! Holy shit…"). Fit is qualitative; counts drift with deletions.

**Use rule:** compute BPR over any 2–4 unit window before editing. If BPR >12 with no fragment/joke/hedge/interjection, split one unit or add a ≤6-word punch. If BPR <2 outside staged contempt/shock, rejoin one period-fragment. Ellipsis >3 dots reserved for unsayable content (S14); elsewhere cap at 3.

## 8. Negative evidence (carried + extended)

## 9. Synthesis for the skill

> Punch (≤6) → unpack (10–18, one prop) → stop. ~1/3 sentences open And/But/So/When/If. Reasons live in because/when/if-tails (T2). Verb-first + to/and/then-tails (T4). Analogy *is* the definition (T8). Payoffs quoted (T7); receipts stacked then walkout (T10); ruminations loop present-tense (T11); confessions flat (T12). Under load, break punctuation first: ellipsis-stall + quiet restart (T13), period-staccato → dash-release (T14), serial EDIT diary (T15). Check any window with BPR (§7): split above 12, rejoin below 2 outside staged shock. End on joke/thanks/number/quote/Edit — never a summary.
