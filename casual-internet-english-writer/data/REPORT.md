# Casual internet English research report

The inherited Reddit-focused package was the main substantive evidence base for the initial casual skill split. Its strongest usable contribution is qualitative: direct participation, active agency, concrete detail, local hedging, flexible rhythm, and artifact-specific differences between posts and replies.

The old composition pipeline underused those observations by applying one grammar-forward cleanup pass to professional and casual artifacts. The split should let the casual skill preserve harmless fragments, simple conjunctions, asides, and uneven cadence while still rejecting forced slang and fabricated errors.

Historical templates and anti-pattern families are retained as optional editorial diagnostics. Fixed word counts, connector rates, mandatory endings, numerical rhythm formulas, and universal absence claims remain non-operational because the raw ledger and item-level verification are unavailable.

During that earlier pass, Reddit verification was attempted but blocked anonymously. Candidate pages and search snippets were not cited as fetched evidence. That limitation describes the earlier retrieval attempt, not the subsequently supplied update corpus below.

## Current revision: from observations to editing decisions

The user-confirmed update corpus adds community reports, editorial research, and 47 pre-2020 Reddit entries across technical discussion and Q&A. Its source map is recorded as U1–U9 in [sources](SOURCES.md). The research was used directly; no new web investigation was needed.

The main operational gains are:

- **Repair clusters, not isolated words.** Empty framing, inflated vocabulary, invented contrasts, significance tails, and repeated conclusions now have explicit replacements and exceptions in the register guide. [U1, U3, U4, U8]
- **Choose the contribution's shape.** A one-line reaction, personal story, and technical explanation do not need the same length or structure. Historical agreement markers, asides, and expert explanations inform these distinctions. [U2, U5, U6]
- **Ground without inventing.** Use a supplied event, limitation, question, or error. Specificity is an information decision, not permission to add dates, metrics, or lived experience. [U2, U4, U8]
- **Use rhythm functionally.** The reported variation motivates breaking monotonous sentence jobs and buried qualifications, not imposing a short sentence in every paragraph. The historical entries include both compact reactions and extended explanations. [U5, U6]

The calibration examples cover cluster removal, supplied grounding, real contrast, actual agreement, narrative flow, technical inventory, speaker separation, and sparse input. They are constructed editorial fixtures, not copied biographies or measured model outputs.

The historical sample's high-engagement and community boundaries remain useful context when choosing register. The supplied observations are the evidence base; universal bans and numerical composition targets are separate editorial decisions, explained in [notes](NOTES.md). No detector performance or runtime A/B improvement is claimed.
